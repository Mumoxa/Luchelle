Facts about Natalie du Toit

  • Encyclopaedia Britannica — Natalie du Toit (born Cape Town, 29 January 1984; accident 2001; left leg amputated at the knee; first female amputee at an Olympic Games; five Paralympic golds in Beijing; flag bearer)
  • ESPN / Associated Press (3 May 2008) (qualified for Beijing 2008 in the 10 km open water; first amputee in an able-bodied major final, 800 m freestyle, 2002 Commonwealth Games)
  • The Independent — “If I can achieve a dream, then anyone can” (scooter accident on 25 February 2001, aged 17; 16th place in the Olympic 10 km)
  • Paralympic.org — The day Natalie du Toit made Olympic history
